PEOPLE & PARTICIPATION

Find shared purpose. Explore challenging problems together.

S2E welcomes researchers, developers, engineers and domain experts to collaborate on difficult problems of shared interest and contribute through verifiable research tasks.

Full-timeInternshipsCollaboration tasksRemote participation

Shared values

Explore with rigorous curiosity

Independent thinking

Explore complex problems with curiosity, form independent judgments and keep learning to identify research questions worth testing.

Personal initiative

Take initiative, keep learning through open questions and take responsibility for concrete outcomes.

Shared interests

Bring a sustained interest in the platform’s long-term questions and a willingness to work across disciplines.

Scientific rigor

Test hypotheses against evidence, value reproducibility and outcome validation, and revise judgments as research progresses.

Participation formats

Contribute through research tasks

Full-time roles

Contribute to core research, engineering, collaboration infrastructure and long-term directions.

Research internships

Begin with a well-defined task and build experience through research, development or validation.

Collaboration tasks

Contribute as an independent researcher, engineer or team through methods, code, validation results or domain expertise.

Cross-city, cross-institution and remote collaboration are supported. Public collaboration locations include Hangzhou, Qingdao, Shenzhen, Hong Kong and Singapore.

  • Representative projects, papers, code or validation results
