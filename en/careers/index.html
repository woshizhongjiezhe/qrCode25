Talent profile

We look for researchers and engineers who can turn difficult problems into enduring system capabilities.

Take on difficult problems

Face hard problems directly, refine algorithms, systems and experimental loops over the long term, and keep making progress in complex environments.

Stay problem-centered

Go beyond completing a task by continually examining its causes, boundary conditions and opportunities for system improvement.

Combine research and engineering

Bring rigorous engineering ability while forming hypotheses, designing experiments, reading research literature and iterating existing approaches to turn ideas into verifiable systems.

Keep iterating

Continue learning in open environments, refine understanding and methods through real feedback, and advance system capabilities over time.

Ways to participate

Three paths, one task standard

Full-time

For partners who can sustain core research or engineering responsibility.

Internship

For focused work on a clear question with a verifiable outcome.

Collaboration task

For independent researchers, engineers or teams working within a defined task boundary.

Remote collaboration

Office city is unrestricted. Public collaboration locations span five cities and regions.

Useful information to include

  • The research or engineering question you care about;
  • one or two projects, papers, repositories or works that show your judgment;
  • your preferred participation format and available time.
