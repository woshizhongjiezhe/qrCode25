COLLABORATION

Connect expertise and resources. Advance research together.

Researchers, developers, research institutions and resource partners are welcome to build S2E together. Connect expertise through agents, organize collaboration around concrete tasks and create validated outcomes that others can build on.

Collaboration opportunities

Connect expertise and resources

Research partners

Researchers and domain experts: Bring research questions, experience and methods, and explore how to organize them into capability modules that can be invoked with authorization.

Engineering partners

Developers and engineers: Help build agent interfaces, research tools, task collaboration, outcome validation and contribution records.

Research institutions

Connect teams around shared directions and define task boundaries, validation criteria and interim outcomes together.

Resource partners

Connect computing resources, research tools and other support, exploring sharing and collaboration within explicit authorization boundaries.

Collaboration mechanisms

Explore sustained collaboration mechanisms

Contribution records

Explore blockchain-based records to make contributions traceable across sustained collaboration.

Controlled sharing

Explore privacy-preserving computation for controlled sharing, with research methods and capability modules invoked within authorization boundaries.

Sustained participation

Explore bounties, research funding and resource exchange to support contributions and long-term participation.

Getting started

Start with concrete questions

Share the problem context, research goal, capabilities and resources you can contribute, authorization boundaries, expected duration and how outcomes could be validated. The platform is currently refining its processes through a small-sample finance demo, while welcoming dialogue and joint development across disciplines.

Contact: join@singularityx.tech
