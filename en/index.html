SINGULARITY TO EVOLUTION

S2E · A distributed AI collaboration platform

S2E (Singularity to Evolution) is a distributed AI collaboration platform under development. It aims to connect researchers, domain experts, engineers, agents and computing resources worldwide, enabling sustained collaboration on difficult problems shared by humanity.

LONG-TERM QUESTIONS

Three shared research challenges

Connect distributed expertise around difficult problems shared by humanity, and begin long-term exploration with concrete tasks.

Energy & living space

How can humanity access more abundant energy and extend sustainable living beyond Earth?

Life & intelligence

How can humanity reduce the limitations imposed by disease and aging, and deepen its understanding of life, cognition and consciousness?

Collaboration & civilization

How can people, agents, knowledge and resources distributed around the world develop a sustained capacity to solve complex problems?

Break long-term ambitions into verifiable research tasks. Fusion research can begin with a control or materials question; brain–computer interfaces with decoding stability; and space-based computing with thermal management and cost models.

HOW WE COLLABORATE

Connect capabilities through collaboration

Connect expertise through agents, organize collaboration around research tasks and support participation through contribution records.

01

Agent capabilities

Organize expert experience and research methods into capability modules that can be invoked with authorization, connecting knowledge, tools and computing resources around a task.

02

Research tasks

Define research goals and validation methods for a concrete question, and begin with outcomes that can be examined and reproduced.

03

Contribution records

Explore blockchain-based contribution records and privacy-preserving computation for controlled sharing, with bounties, research funding and resource exchange to support sustained participation.

CURRENT STAGE

Validate through financial research

Start with a small-sample finance demo to refine task collaboration, outcome validation and contribution records.

Current demo

Finance validation

S2E is currently using a small-sample demo in finance to refine task collaboration, outcome validation and contribution records, gradually developing the capabilities needed to expand into other scientific fields.

  1. 01

    Task collaboration

    Define goals, responsibilities and testable assumptions around a concrete financial research question.

  2. 02

    Outcome validation

    Use research methods and reproducible evidence to assess whether intermediate results support the original assumptions.

  3. 03

    Contribution records

    Record participation across research activities and outcomes to provide a traceable basis for future collaboration.

Gradual expansion

Build experience through the finance demo, then gradually explore collaboration in other scientific fields.
