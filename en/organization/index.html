Organization

Organize talent through tasks, and direction through long-term questions.

Collaboration is defined by problems, roles, milestones and public interfaces rather than a single-office boundary.

Collaboration design

Small teams, clear interfaces, staged validation

  • Long-term questions
    Directions remain continuous while tasks change with evidence.
  • Small teams
    Compact groups form around a specific problem.
  • Open interfaces
    Full-time, internship and collaboration tasks run in parallel.
  • Cross-city and remote
    Office city is unrestricted and remote work is supported.

Public collaboration locations

Hangzhou, Qingdao, Shenzhen, Hong Kong and Singapore. Together, these locations form an open network for cross-city, cross-institution and remote collaboration.
