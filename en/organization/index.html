SINGULARITY TO EVOLUTION

Connect global expertise. Build an open research ecosystem.

S2E (Singularity to Evolution) is a distributed AI collaboration platform under development. It aims to connect researchers, domain experts, engineers, agents and computing resources worldwide, enabling sustained collaboration on difficult problems shared by humanity.

Under developmentAgents as the interfaceVerifiable research tasks
Long-term questions

Connect expertise across disciplines

Energy & living space: How can humanity access more abundant energy and extend sustainable living beyond Earth?

Life & intelligence: How can humanity reduce the limitations imposed by disease and aging, and deepen its understanding of life, cognition and consciousness?

Collaboration & civilization: How can people, agents, knowledge and resources distributed around the world develop a sustained capacity to solve complex problems?

Collaboration interface

Coordinate expertise through agents

S2E uses agents as the interface for collaboration. It organizes expert experience and research methods into capability modules that can be invoked with authorization, and breaks long-term ambitions into verifiable research tasks.

Fusion research can begin with a control or materials question; brain–computer interfaces with decoding stability; and space-based computing with thermal management and cost models.

Sustained participation

Explore sustained collaboration mechanisms

The platform is exploring blockchain-based contribution records and privacy-preserving computation for controlled sharing, alongside bounties, research funding and resource exchange to support sustained participation.

Current stage

Validate through financial research

S2E is currently using a small-sample demo in finance to refine task collaboration, outcome validation and contribution records, gradually developing the capabilities needed to expand into other scientific fields.

Build together

Support public research collaboration

S2E is guided by public research and community collaboration in the public interest. We value strong reasoning, self-direction, shared interests and an evidence-based mindset, and welcome researchers, developers, research institutions and resource partners.

Shared challenges give us direction. Verifiable research tasks make collaboration possible.

Let one person’s important discovery become a starting point for others to explore further.
