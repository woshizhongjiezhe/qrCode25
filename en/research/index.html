RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

Frame shared challenges. Develop verifiable research tasks.

S2E focuses on three long-term questions, organizing sustained collaboration around concrete problems, research methods and validation evidence. Shared challenges provide direction; each task provides a way to begin.

Energy & living spaceLife & intelligenceCollaboration & civilization

Long-term directions

Three shared research challenges

Energy & living space

How can humanity access more abundant energy and extend sustainable living beyond Earth?

Life & intelligence

How can humanity reduce the limitations imposed by disease and aging, and deepen its understanding of life, cognition and consciousness?

Collaboration & civilization

How can people, agents, knowledge and resources distributed around the world develop a sustained capacity to solve complex problems?

Task examples

Define verifiable research tasks

These examples illustrate how long-term directions can be broken into tasks. The scope and validation criteria for each task need to be defined with collaborators.

Fusion research

Begin with a control or materials question, defining a research hypothesis and a way to evaluate it.

Brain–computer interfaces

Begin with decoding stability, translating a long-term goal into a specific question that can be compared and validated.

Space-based computing

Begin with thermal management and cost models, working with testable models, constraints and assumptions.

Current validation

Validate through financial research

S2E is currently using a small-sample demo in finance to refine task collaboration, outcome validation and contribution records, gradually developing the capabilities needed to expand into other scientific fields.

The current priority is to refine task collaboration, outcome validation and contribution records, while gradually building the capabilities needed for other scientific fields.
